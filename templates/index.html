COMMON INQUIRIES

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How frequently should I get my water tank cleaned?

According to health standards, residential water storage tanks should be cleaned every 4 to 6 months to prevent the accumulation of mud, algae, and hazardous bacterial colonies.

Do I need to empty the tank before the DRTC team arrives?

No! Our technicians bring high-flow submersible dewatering drainage pumps. It is helpful if water levels are low, but we handle the complete dewatering and sludge removal on-site.

Are the antibacterial chemicals safe for drinking water?

Yes, 100%! We strictly use certified food-grade, chlorine-free and eco-friendly disinfectants followed by UV germicidal radiation that leave zero chemical residue, color, or toxic aftertaste.

How long does the 6-stage cleaning take?

A standard 1000L plastic tank takes approximately 45–60 minutes. Larger 3000L or 5000L tanks take between 90 to 120 minutes.

When and how do I make payment?

You only pay AFTER you inspect the cleaned tank and are 100% satisfied. You can pay via Cash, PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm directly to Dinesh or Rakesh.
