2. Scope of Scientific Mechanized Services

DRTC provides mechanized hygiene cleaning for domestic, commercial, and industrial water storage reservoirs (Overhead Sintex/PVC Tanks, Underground RCC Concrete Sumps, and Mega Industrial Reservoirs up to 1,50,000 Liters). Our standard 6-stage scientific process encompasses:

  1. Stage 1 — Mechanized Dewatering: Evacuation of stagnant bottom water using submersible electric drainage pumps.
  2. Stage 2 — Sludge & Silt Extraction: Industrial wet-slurry vacuum extraction of settled mud, silt, rust flakes, and organic matter.
  3. Stage 3 — High-Pressure Jet Washing: Rotary rotary-jet scrubbing of internal tank walls, ceiling, corners, and base with high-pressure water wands.
  4. Stage 4 — Vacuum Residue Evacuation: Pumping out dislodged dirt slurry and residue water.
  5. Stage 5 — Anti-Bacterial Bio-Sanitization: Spraying certified non-toxic, food-grade biodegradable anti-bacterial agents to eliminate micro-pathogens and fungal spores.
  6. Stage 6 — Germicidal UV-C Disinfection: Final UV-C ultraviolet radiation exposure eliminating suspended biological bacteria and algae spores.
