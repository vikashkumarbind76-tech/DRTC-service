Privacy Policy

Last Updated: {{ last_updated }} • Applicable to DRTC Service (Dinesh Rakesh Tank Cleaning)

1. Introduction

At DRTC Service ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and trust. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website, book mechanized water tank cleaning services, or contact our dispatch team.

2. Information We Collect

When you schedule a cleaning appointment or submit an inquiry, we collect only necessary operational details, including:

  • Contact Information: Full name, 10-digit mobile number, and optional email address.
  • Service Location: House/flat number, building name, street address, locality, landmark, and pincode in Gamharia, Adityapur, or Jamshedpur.
  • Service Specifications: Tank capacity (in liters), count of tanks, preferred appointment date, and time slot.
  • Communications: Call logs or WhatsApp conversation context initiated to verify cleaning access.

3. Purpose of Data Collection

Your information is used strictly to fulfill your water tank cleaning service, specifically to:

  • Dispatch certified cleaning technicians (Dinesh & Rakesh) to your accurate premises.
  • Send booking confirmations and status updates via SMS, Phone, or WhatsApp.
  • Provide authentic booking receipts and warranty support.
  • Improve our local service quality and customer support operations.

4. Strict No-Sale & Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details, phone numbers, or addresses to any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or aggregators under any circumstances. Data is exclusively accessed by our direct field dispatch team.

5. Data Retention & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption and access controls to ensure your records are protected against unauthorized access. Records of completed service appointments are retained only for maintenance recall verification and operational billing audits.
