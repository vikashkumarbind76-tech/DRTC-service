Privacy Policy & Personal Data Protection

Effective Date: {{ last_updated }} • Governing Law: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 & IT Act, 2000 (India) • Entity: DRTC Service, Gamharia

1. Preamble & Statutory Applicability

This Privacy Policy and Personal Data Protection Charter ("Policy") constitutes a legally binding document between you ("User", "Customer", or "Data Principal") and DRTC Service (operating as Dinesh Rakesh Tank Cleaning Service, hereinafter referred to as "DRTC", "the Enterprise", "we", "our", or "us").

This Policy is framed in strict compliance with the statutory provisions of:

  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, 2023) and applicable rules notified thereunder;
  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act), specifically Sections 43A, 72, and 72A (penalties for disclosure of information in breach of lawful contract);
  • The Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules); and
  • The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 read alongside the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

By accessing our platform, generating an instant quote, placing a service booking, communicating via telephone, SMS, or WhatsApp, or permitting our certified cleaning technicians onto your premises, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and unequivocally consented to the collection, lawful processing, and protection of your personal data as governed by this Policy.

2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

In accordance with the principle of Data Minimization under Section 6 of the DPDP Act, 2023, DRTC collects only such personal information as is strictly necessary to fulfill your mechanized water tank cleaning request and ensure worker safety:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full name of the customer or authorized facility manager, primary 10-digit mobile telephone number, secondary contact number (if provided), and electronic mail (email) address.
  • Physical Geo-Location & Premises Address: House/flat number, apartment/society name, street name, nearest prominent landmark, locality (e.g., Gamharia, Adityapur, Bistupur, Sonari, Kadma, Telco, Jugsalai), town, and postal PIN code (832108 / 831001 etc.).
  • Tank & Service Technical Data: Tank capacity in liters (500L up to 1,50,000L), tank type (Overhead Sintex/PVC or Underground RCC Concrete Sump), number of units, preferred date and time slot, and photographs of the tank or terrace access voluntarily shared via WhatsApp for preliminary technical assessment.
  • Transactional & Fulfillment Logs: Unique DRTC Service Tracking ID (e.g., DRTC-9821), date and time of dispatch, assigned technician name, service completion timestamp, customer feedback, and physical verification sign-off.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser user-agent string, and submission timestamps recorded solely for cybersecurity monitoring, automated bot defense, and verification against malicious or fraudulent bookings.

Financial Data Disclaimer: DRTC never stores, processes, or captures bank account passwords, debit/credit card CVV numbers, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs. All payments are strictly peer-to-peer (P2P UPI or on-site Cash on Delivery) processed through official NPCI-licensed UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) directly to authorized accounts.

3. Lawful Basis & Specific Purpose of Data Processing

Under Indian law, your personal data is processed strictly under the lawful basis of your explicit consent and legitimate operational necessity:

  • Technician Dispatch & Field Routing: Directing certified cleaning technicians (Dinesh Kumar & Rakesh Kumar) with industrial pressure-wash equipment directly to your premises without delay.
  • Operational Verification & Access Check: Contacting you 1–2 hours prior to arrival via phone call or WhatsApp to confirm power supply availability (220V socket) and water drainage permissions.
  • Warranty & Satisfaction Tracking: Verifying your unique Booking Tracking ID to honor our 48-Hour Free Re-Cleaning Satisfaction Guarantee.
  • Receipt Generation & Invoicing: Issuing transparent electronic service receipts and GST-compliant service documentation for commercial/society clients.
  • Prevention of Fraudulent & Hoax Bookings: Protecting our field technicians against fake dispatch addresses, malicious hoax bookings, and harassment under Sections 336 / 415 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).

4. Absolute Prohibition on Data Sale & Third-Party Sharing

DRTC maintains a strict zero-commercialization policy regarding personal data:

  • We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, barter, or distribute your personal phone numbers, addresses, or booking histories to third-party telemarketing companies, lead aggregators, digital ad networks, or data brokers.
  • Access to your contact details and address is restricted strictly to our direct operations leads and dispatched field personnel who are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Statutory Disclosures: Personal information will only be disclosed to Indian law enforcement authorities, Cyber Crime Cells, or judicial courts when compelled by a valid statutory notice, warrant, or court order under the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC) / Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS).

5. Security Safeguards & IT Act Rule 8 Compliance

In accordance with Rule 8 of the Information Technology (SPDI) Rules, 2011 and Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act, 2023, DRTC enforces comprehensive technical and operational safeguards:

  • Cryptographic Data In Transit: All communication between your web browser and our servers is secured with 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) encryption.
  • Protected Database Architecture: Backend database records are hosted on enterprise cloud infrastructure (PostgreSQL with SSL mode required) protected by automated firewalls, isolated VPC networks, and stringent role-based credential controls.
  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) & XSS Defense: Django cryptographic token protection on every booking form preventing unauthorized submission injection.
  • Data Breach Protocol: In the unforeseen event of a security compromise impacting personal data, DRTC shall notify affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board of India in accordance with statutory guidelines.

6. Data Retention Period & Deletion Protocol

Personal data collected for service fulfillment is retained only for the period necessary to:

  • Honor the 48-Hour Satisfaction Guarantee and provide periodic bi-annual or annual tank maintenance recall notifications requested by the customer;
  • Maintain operational and financial records as mandated by Indian tax and accounting regulations.

Once the retention purpose is achieved, records are either securely purged from production databases or irreversibly anonymized for aggregate statistical auditing.

7. Rights of the Data Principal

As a Data Principal under Indian law, you are entitled to exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal data and booking history processed by DRTC.
  • Right to Correction & Updation: Request correction of any inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete phone numbers or service addresses.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the complete deletion of your booking records and contact information, subject to statutory retention obligations under tax laws.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for non-essential service notifications by contacting our Grievance Officer.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Avail of an accessible grievance redressal mechanism regarding your privacy rights.

8. Protection of Minors

Our services and web portal are strictly intended for property owners, authorized adult tenants, residential welfare association (RWA) executives, or facility administrators aged 18 years or older. DRTC does not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has scheduled an appointment without parental or legal guardian consent, the booking and related data will be cancelled and deleted immediately.
